      How to play
      • Everyone starts on 31 and counts down. The first to hit exactly 0 wins.
      • Throw your toads at the table: one in the hole scores 2, one left on the table scores 1, one on the floor scores 0.
      • Toads per turn: 2 in your first turn, 3 in your second, then 4 (never more than the points you need).
      • Bust: score more than you need and your score stays where it was.
      • Before each game everyone throws one toad: closest to the hole throws first. In a tournament, a match is first to 2 games.

      Scoring: tap the toad with the points the thrower made. A toad turns brass when that score finishes the game.
